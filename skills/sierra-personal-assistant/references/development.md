# Reflection and learning

Use the person's confirmed growing edge, not a fixed alignment module. Analyze approved evidence and the person's corrections. Reflect on behaviors under their control, without scoring productivity, ranking peers or diagnosing personality.

## Adapt the lens

- **Alignment & Communication:** look for timely proposals, dependency checks, clear updates and closed loops. A quiet channel alone does not prove missing alignment.
- **Delegation & Empowerment:** look for explicit ownership, clarity of desired outcome, decision boundaries and agreed checkpoints. Retaining a task can be appropriate when scope, urgency or authority prevents delegation. Do not suggest transferring work to someone without evidence of their role or capacity.
- **Strategic Thinking:** connect implementation choices to stated outcomes, trade-offs and assumptions. Do not invent business goals or access metrics outside the approved scope.
- **Other edges:** ask which behaviors and signals matter, then adapt the same evidence-to-action method.

## Weekly reflection

Provide the reviewed interval and source coverage, concrete examples of progress, a possible missed opportunity with uncertainty, and relevant counterevidence or contextual constraints. If the sources do not support progress or a missed opportunity, say that evidence is insufficient; do not fill every category with guesses.

Suggest one small experiment for the next week: trigger, behavior to try, observable success signal and review date. Example: “For the next change affecting another workstream, share two options before implementation and note whether the dependency became clearer.” This is a practice target, not an employee score.

For learning, suggest a practice question or a short exercise tied to a real challenge. Ask before researching paid courses or introducing another service; using this skill does not authorize purchases or signups.

Invite corrections. Update the private profile only for confirmed changes; do not rewrite a long-term edge from one noisy week. Review automation relevance when the edge changes, and obtain approval before changing schedules, source scope or delivery. Keep the reflection private.
