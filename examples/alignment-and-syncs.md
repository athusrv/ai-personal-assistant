# Fictional alignment and sync examples

## Before implementation

**Supplied context:** a fictional planning note says a proposed retry change may affect another workstream. It does not show approval or a deadline.

**Private recommendation:** “The planning note identifies a dependency. Before implementation, ask the responsible workstream owner whether the approach affects their work. I cannot tell from this note whether that conversation already happened.”

**Draft:** “I am considering changing the retry behavior. I see a possible dependency with your workstream. Before I implement, is there an assumption or constraint I should account for?”

Status: draft only. No recipient was inferred and no message was sent. The private development profile is excluded from the draft.

## Prepare a sync

**User:** “Prepare my next project sync from the notes I supplied.”

**Private note:**

- Purpose: resolve the approach and identify dependencies.
- Progress: the supplied fictional notes describe a proposed retry change, not a completed rollout.
- Open question: who owns the dependency check?
- Decision needed: which constraints should shape the implementation?
- Agenda: confirm the goal; discuss options; assign a follow-up with an agreed due date.
- Growing-edge practice: invite input before presenting the approach as final.
- Coverage: supplied notes only; no calendar or Slack access was verified.

Offer a shareable agenda without the coaching line. Do not post it, edit the meeting notes or create a calendar event without explicit approval.
