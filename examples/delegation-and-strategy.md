# Fictional personalization examples

## Delegation & Empowerment

**Goal:** reduce unnecessary centralization while helping the team own outcomes.

**Morning lens:** which upcoming tasks could benefit from clearer ownership? Check explicit roles and constraints before suggesting a delegate; do not assume capacity.

**Evening lens:** did the person define an outcome and decision boundary, or take back work without clarifying expectations? Mark missing context and legitimate constraints.

**Weekly experiment:** on one suitable task, agree on the owner, expected outcome and checkpoint, then ask what support is needed. Discuss evidence with the person; do not score the teammate.

## Strategic Thinking

**Goal:** connect technical choices with stated project outcomes.

**Morning lens:** identify one upcoming decision whose outcome or success measure is unclear.

**Evening lens:** identify an assumption or trade-off to validate, using only retrieved objectives.

**Weekly experiment:** before one consequential choice, write the expected outcome, two options and the assumption that would change the decision. Ask for context when business goals are absent; do not invent them.

These lenses change the substance of the recurring analysis. They are not alignment prompts with a different growing-edge label. All routines remain proposals until configured and verified for the individual.
