# Fictional onboarding from written feedback

All material below is invented and contains no employee information.

**User:** “Here is a written feedback excerpt. Help me choose a growing edge.”

**Feedback, section 1:** “Your explanations after delivery are clear. On two cross-team changes we only learned about the approach once implementation was complete. Earlier discussion would have helped us identify a dependency.”

**Expected analysis:**

- Observation: the excerpt describes clear post-delivery explanations and two late discussions (section 1).
- Hypothesis: earlier alignment on changes affecting others may be useful. Another explanation could be unclear ownership or expectations; this excerpt does not establish a general trait.
- Proposed focus: Alignment & Communication.
- Practice: share the proposed approach and a specific dependency question before implementation.
- Success signal: relevant input is sought while the decision is still changeable.
- Confirmation: “Does this fit your experience, or would you choose a different focus?”

**After confirmation:** agree on private persistence and source boundaries, then propose a morning preparation, end-of-day communication review and weekly reflection. Ask which to enable and confirm schedule/time zone/private delivery. If no scheduler exists, clearly state “not active” and provide prompts.

**Expected limitations:** do not infer the person's overall performance, store the raw feedback automatically, publish it, or send a colleague a message. If the document cannot be read, request a relevant excerpt rather than inventing evidence.

**Changing focus:** “My priority is now delegation.” The agent proposes updated practices and success signals, asks for confirmation before changing the private profile, and reviews affected routine prompts without silently changing their schedule.
